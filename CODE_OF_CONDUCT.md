# Code of Conduct

ChalkPilot contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to
create a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and learning
  needs.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the safety boundaries of camera and voice data.
- Accept correction and repair harm when something goes wrong.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
privacy violations, and sustained disruption are not tolerated in project
spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through GitHub. The
maintainer may remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors when
necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical.

Enforcement decisions should prioritize safety, proportionality, and a clear
path to repair where appropriate.
